# Setup Guide

This workflow is designed for paid AI accounts with Project, workspace, or similar features and higher file-upload limits. It is intended for organizing records in one persistent place rather than scattering uploads across unrelated chats.

This is not a recommendation to buy a subscription. Free accounts may work for testing one or two small files only, such as one decision letter, one DBQ, one pathology report, one sleep study, or a small group of records. Do not rely on a free account for a full C-File, full VA Blue Button report, or years of private records.

If cost is a barrier, consider free assistance from an accredited Veterans Service Organization, county Veterans Service Officer, state veterans department, or VA-accredited representative.

## Basic Setup

1. Create a new Project or workspace in ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI tool with similar file and context features.
2. Paste the prompt from [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md) as the first message.
3. Upload records into that same Project or workspace.
4. Keep record review in that Project or workspace so the AI can maintain continuity.
5. Keep original files unchanged.

## Before Uploading

Before uploading records, check the current file type, file size, file count, and project limits of the AI platform. These limits change.

Consider redacting:

* SSN
* VA file number
* full date of birth
* address
* phone number
* unrelated third-party information

Do not upload another person's records without permission.

## File Preparation

Upload original PDFs where possible. Avoid screenshots unless no PDF exists.

Name files clearly where possible. Helpful names may include a date range, source, or record type, such as:

* `VA-Blue-Button-2020-2025.pdf`
* `Private-Orthopedics-2023.pdf`
* `STR-Separation-Exam.pdf`
* `VA-Rating-Decision-2024.pdf`

Do not alter original files. If you create redacted copies, keep the original files separately and unchanged.

## Recommended Upload Checklist

Do not worry if you do not have every item. Upload what you have and let the prompt identify what appears missing.

* DD-214 or separation documents
* service dates if DD-214 is unavailable
* military service treatment records / STRs
* separation exam and Report of Medical History, such as DD 2807 / DD 2808 if available
* military personnel records
* deployment records
* Line of Duty determinations
* toxic-exposure records if applicable
* VA Blue Button / My HealtheVet records from VA.gov
* VA treatment records
* private medical records
* specialist records
* imaging reports
* lab reports
* pathology reports
* sleep studies
* EMG/NCS reports
* vascular studies
* pulmonary function tests
* surgical records
* medication lists
* C&P exam reports
* DBQs
* nexus letters
* lay statements or buddy statements
* prior VA rating decisions
* VA decision letters
* VA code sheets
* full C-File if available

## Working With Large Record Sets

Large record sets may need to be reviewed in logical batches. The prompt instructs the AI to manage this by file, record type, provider, or date range.

The AI should clearly state which files were reviewed, which files were not reviewed yet, and whether the inventory is complete or partial.

Do not rely on an inventory that does not identify reviewed and unreviewed files.
