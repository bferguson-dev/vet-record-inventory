# Disclaimer

This project is for educational and record-organization purposes only.

It does not provide:

* legal advice
* medical advice
* VA claim representation
* diagnosis
* nexus opinions
* DBQs
* VA rating predictions
* claim recommendations

The materials in this repository are intended to help users organize what appears in uploaded records. They do not decide VA eligibility, establish medical causation, recommend filing strategy, or replace qualified professional help.

Users must verify all findings against the original records. AI tools can miss evidence, misread records, misunderstand context, or hallucinate unsupported facts. An AI-generated inventory should be treated as a draft that requires human review.

Users should consult qualified professionals when appropriate, including medical providers, VA-accredited representatives, Veterans Service Organizations, attorneys, or claims agents.

Users are responsible for protecting their own private information. Veteran records may contain SSN, full date of birth, VA file number, addresses, phone numbers, mental-health records, medical records, medication history, and other sensitive information. Consider redaction and review the privacy, data-retention, and training-use settings of any AI platform before uploading records.

Do not upload another person's records without permission.

The project maintainers are not responsible for VA claim outcomes, medical decisions, legal decisions, financial decisions, or any other user decisions made after using these materials.
